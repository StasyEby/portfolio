End-to-end digital banking product delivery across mobile and web platforms, account opening, payments, lending, customer experience and banking operations.

My Roles: Product Manager | Business Analyst | Project Lead | Quality Assurance

Delivery Status: Deployed & Currently Supported

Project Overview

Contributed across product management, business analysis, project leadership and quality assurance for a comprehensive digital banking solution delivered across Android, iOS and web platforms.

The solution brings together digital account opening, customer verification, banking transactions, lending, customer self-service, rewards and operational management capabilities within an integrated fintech environment.

The platforms have been deployed and are currently under ongoing support.

Business and Product Objectives

  • Support digital access to banking services across mobile and web.
  • Digitise customer account opening and verification processes.
  • Provide convenient access to transfers and payment services.
  • Enable digital loan application and management.
  • Improve customer access to account information and self-service.
  • Introduce rewards and customer engagement capabilities.
  • Support operational oversight, security and audit management.
