The Business Challenge

The client required a more structured approach to managing customer information, property-related records, payment activities, customer follow-ups and business reporting.

The project addressed the need for a centralised digital platform that could support more organised customer relationship management and improve visibility into operational activities.

Project Objectives

  • Centralise customer information and management.
  • Improve payment tracking and receipt management.
  • Introduce structured customer payment reminders.
  • Provide dashboard visibility into business activities.
  • Support more organised customer relationship processes.

Solution & Key Functionalities

Customer Management

Centralised customer records and CRM management functionality.

Payment Tracking

Payment receipt management, with amounts received highlighted in bold red for visibility.

Automated Reminders

Customer reminders scheduled at 11, 7, 3 and 1 days ahead of relevant payment dates.

Operational Dashboard

Dashboard and management modules supporting operational visibility.

Delivery & Implementation

The CRM solution progressed through requirements coordination, product implementation, functional validation, deployment and client support.

The completed solution was deployed and transitioned into active client use.

Business Value Delivered

The implementation established a centralised platform for customer and payment management, introduced structured reminder processes and provided improved visibility into customer-related operational activities.
